Mango Matcha

by Nami

Mango matcha is a modern fusion beverage that combines ceremonial-grade matcha, a finely ground Japanese green tea, with sweet mango purée or syrup. Key ingredients: Ceremonial grade matcha powder, fresh mango puree, milk, water, ice.

Ingredients

  • Mango layer:
  • 1/2 ripe mango, peeled and pureed (100g)
  • 1 tsp honey or agave
  • Matcha layer:
  • 2g ceremonial-grade matcha powder
  • 30–60 ml (2–4 tbsp) hot water
  • Milk layer:
  • (180–240 ml) cold milk of choice (oat milk recommended)
  • Ice to fill the glass

Directions

  1. Peel and chop 1/2 ripe mango.
  2. Blend with 1–2 tbsp cold water until completely smooth and silky. If it's too thick, add a little more water.
  3. Spoon the puree into the bottom of a tall glass (about 1/4 full).
  4. Shif 2g of matcha powder into a small bowl.
  5. Add 1 tbsp hot water and whisk vigorously in a W or M motion for 15–20 seconds until smooth and frothy.
  6. Add the remaining 2–3 tbsp hot water and whisk again until fully dissolved.
  7. Fill the glass with ice cubes about 1/2 full. Slow pour 3/4-1 cup of cold milk.
  8. Gently pour the whisked matcha over the milk. It will float on top, creating the signature three-layer look (mango → milk → matcha).
  9. Stir well before drinking to combine all three layers.

Delicious and easy to make. The mangoes make a huge difference in the drink's flavor.
